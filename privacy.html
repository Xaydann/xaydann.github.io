Stardoku — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026. This policy applies to the Stardoku app for iOS.

The short version. Your puzzle progress never leaves your phone — we have no servers and no accounts. The one exception is advertising: Stardoku shows ads between puzzles, and Google's advertising software, which is built into the app, collects device information in order to serve them. You can decline personalised advertising and the whole game still works.

1. Who we are

Stardoku publishes Stardoku. If you want to reach a person about anything on this page, write to stardokulogicpuzzle@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you do in the game is stored locally, in the app's own storage on your phone. None of it is transmitted to us, because there is nowhere to transmit it to. That includes:

  • levels completed, your Voyage position and your Singularity run
  • stardust, achievements, titles, cosmetics and Observatory purchases
  • your star map, and the date, time and difficulty of every puzzle you solved
  • your Daily Star calendar and streak
  • the display name you type in the wardrobe
  • your settings, including sound, colours and accessibility options

We cannot see any of it. We cannot recover it for you either: if you delete the app, it is gone, and there is no account to restore it from.

3. Advertising

Stardoku is free and is paid for by advertising. Ads appear between puzzles, and optionally when you choose to watch one in exchange for something in the game. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob, whose software is part of the app.

To select and measure ads, Google collects and processes information from your device. Depending on your choices, that can include:

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. What they do with it is governed by their own terms, which we do not control:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob
  • Google's advertising partners

Your choices

  • Tracking. On first launch iOS asks whether Stardoku may track you across other apps and websites. Say no and Google serves non-personalised ads instead. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent, in the UK and EU. If you are in a region covered by GDPR, a consent form appears before any personalised advertising. You may refuse. Refusing does not restrict the game in any way.
  • Turning ads off entirely. Stardoku offers a one-off Remove ads purchase in Settings. Buying it switches off every ad in the game permanently. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details, name or address.

Declining every one of the above still leaves you the whole game: every puzzle, every mode, every achievement and every cosmetic. Nothing is withheld from people who say no.

4. What we do not do

  • We have no user accounts and no login.
  • We run no analytics of our own. We do not know how many puzzles you have solved, or whether you opened the app today.
  • We do not ask for, receive or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any health or financial information.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for money.
  • The game itself makes no network requests. The only network traffic Stardoku generates is Google's, for advertising.

5. iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, Stardoku can copy your save to your own iCloud account so your progress follows you to a new phone. This is optional and can be switched off in Settings → iCloud sync.

That copy lives in your iCloud, not on any server of ours. We cannot read it, and there is no account with us to create or delete. It holds the same things listed in section 2. To remove it, turn off iCloud sync, or delete the app's data from Settings → Apple ID → iCloud on your device.

6. Game Center

Stardoku does not currently use Game Center. If leaderboards are added, your Game Center nickname and your scores would be visible to other players through Apple’s Game Center, governed by Apple’s privacy policy rather than ours. Nothing else would be shared.

7. Children

Stardoku is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Advertising in Stardoku is configured with Google's maximum ad content rating set to G, so ad content is restricted to material suitable for general audiences. If you are a parent and would rather your child saw no advertising at all, decline the tracking prompt, or use Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions to disallow tracking for every app on the device.

8. Your rights

Because we hold nothing about you, there is no data for us to show, correct or delete on request. To erase everything the app has stored on your phone, use Settings → Reset everything inside the game, or delete the app.

For advertising data held by Google, exercise your rights directly with Google using their privacy tools, linked in section 3. If you are in the EU, UK, or a US state with a privacy statute, those rights apply to Google as the controller of that data.

9. Data outside your country

We transfer nothing. Google's advertising infrastructure is global, so advertising data may be processed outside your country under Google's own safeguards.

10. Security

Game data sits in your device's app storage, protected by iOS and by your device passcode. Since nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server of ours that could be breached.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant — for example if we ever began collecting something ourselves — we will say so in the app's release notes rather than quietly editing this page.

12. Contact

stardokulogicpuzzle@gmail.com

Stardoku is a single-player logic puzzle. It has no accounts, no social features and no messaging. This policy covers the iOS app only.
