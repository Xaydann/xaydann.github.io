How the printed version works

On paper the regions are drawn as thick outlines rather than filled colours. That is deliberate: it is the standard for this family of puzzle, it stays readable in black and white, and it does not drink a cartridge of ink. Turn on region letters if you would rather label them.

Every puzzle here is produced by the same engine and verified by the same solver the app uses. Each has exactly one solution and can be finished by deduction alone. Free to use at home, in classrooms and in clubs.
