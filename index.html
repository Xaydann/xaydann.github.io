About

Made by one person, carefully

Stardoku was built alone, over a long time, by someone who wanted a puzzle game that never cheats. There is no energy meter, no countdown pressuring you into a purchase, and nothing that punishes you for putting it down for a week.

Every board is checked by the same solver that teaches you. If the solver cannot finish it by logic alone, you never see it. That is the whole promise, and it is enforced in code rather than hoped for.

The promise, in short

✓  Every puzzle solvable without guessing

✓  No account, no sign-up, no login

✓  Plays fully offline

✓  Cosmetics earned by playing, not bought

✓  Colourblind palette and region letters built in

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I ever have to guess?

No. Every board is verified before it is served: the solver must be able to finish it using deduction alone. If you are stuck, there is always a next move, and Explain will show you which square and why.

Is it free?

Yes. Stardoku is free and shows an advert between puzzles. A single one-off purchase removes them. Every puzzle, mode, achievement and cosmetic is available either way, and stardust can only be earned by playing.

Does it work offline?

Completely. The whole game runs on your device. Your progress is saved there, and optionally to your own iCloud so it follows you to a new phone.

Is this the same as Star Battle or Queens?

It is the same family of logic, yes. Stardoku adds a taught progression of techniques, a solver that can explain any position, and a star map built from everything you have solved.

Can I use the printable puzzles in my classroom?

Please do. They are free for home, classroom and club use. Generate as many as you like on the printables page.

I am colourblind. Can I play?

Yes. There is a palette chosen so no two regions collapse together, and an option to print a letter in each region so colour is never the only cue.
